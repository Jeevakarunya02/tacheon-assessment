# Walkthrough Notes

## Why I Focused on Internal Users First

The primary pain point described in the scenario was internal reporting inefficiency. Because of this, I prioritized internal analysts and account managers as the main users for version 1.

## Why I Excluded AI Features

Although AI-powered recommendations and forecasting could provide additional value, they were intentionally excluded from the first version to avoid unnecessary complexity and ensure faster implementation and easier validation.

## Why I Designed a Centralized Reporting Layer

The assessment specifically mentioned that the company does not want to change its current tools or workflows. Because of this, I designed the system as a centralized analytics layer that integrates with existing marketing platforms instead of replacing them.

## What I Would Improve With More Time

- Real-time analytics support
- Client-facing dashboards
- Automated anomaly detection
- Smarter campaign recommendations
- Historical trend analysis
