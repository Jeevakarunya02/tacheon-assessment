# Product Brief

## Problem Statement

Marketing analysts currently rely on manual workflows to collect campaign data from multiple platforms such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, and analytics tools. This process is time-consuming, inconsistent, and heavily dependent on individual team members.

As a result:
- Reporting takes longer than necessary
- Insights are inconsistent across team members
- Important marketing decisions are delayed
- Teams spend more time collecting data than analyzing it

The company needs a centralized internal tool that makes marketing performance insights faster, more reliable, and easier to access.

## Goal

The goal of this tool is to provide a centralized internal dashboard that helps marketing teams quickly understand campaign performance across channels and identify where attention or optimization is needed.

The tool should:
- Reduce manual reporting effort
- Improve consistency in insights
- Enable faster decision-making
- Provide a simple and reliable user experience

## Primary Users

The primary users for version 1 of the tool are:

1. Marketing Analysts
   - Monitor campaign performance
   - Generate insights quickly
   - Reduce manual reporting effort

2. Account Managers
   - Understand client campaign health
   - Share performance summaries with clients
   - Identify channels that require attention

Clients are intentionally excluded from v1 access to keep the system focused internally while workflows and reporting standards are validated.
